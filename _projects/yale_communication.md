Social navigation in high-stakes spaces such as warehouses is challenging. Multiple robots share the floor, often carrying heavy loads, while the people working around them are cognitively burdened, which makes multimodal interaction difficult.

We are interested in how people perceive different communication modalities from a moving robot.

*This is ongoing work; more details will be shared soon.*
